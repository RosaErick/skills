# Optional exploration

Use questions to open a useful design space, not to collect a mandatory brief.

- Problem: What event makes the current approach fail? Who experiences it?
- Outcome: What would become easier, faster or possible? How would we notice?
- Alternatives: Could a smaller process change solve it? Which two or three approaches have meaningfully different tradeoffs?
- Constraints: Which compatibility, time, budget or operating requirement eliminates an option?
- Risk: Which assumption could overturn the choice? What cheap experiment would test it?

Ask only prompts whose answers are missing and decision-relevant. Summarize a selected direction in the user's terms. Keep useful rejected alternatives as brief rationale when they explain the choice; do not require a formal design document for every idea.
