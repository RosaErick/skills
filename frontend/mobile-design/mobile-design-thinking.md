# Think through the affected interaction

Start from the user's task, actual platform and existing flow. Identify the primary action, information needed to choose it and the feedback that confirms progress. Reuse established decisions rather than filling out a compulsory design matrix.

For a new or uncertain interaction, compare a few options against reachability, discoverability, interruption, accessibility and device constraints. Ask only for missing product decisions that would change the implementation. A simple bug fix may already have all the necessary context.

Evaluate the relevant tradeoff: visible controls versus gestures, dense information versus readability, immediate feedback versus pending work, or consistency with platform expectations versus a product-specific interaction. Provide an accessible alternative to an otherwise undiscoverable or inaccessible essential gesture.

Build and inspect the changed flow. Use component decomposition, interaction sketches or a short design rationale when they clarify a hard decision; none is a form required before every mobile edit.
